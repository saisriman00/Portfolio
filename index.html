// 01 — about me

My Story

I didn't fall in love with data because of the numbers — I fell in love with what hides inside them. Growing up in Kanigiri, Andhra Pradesh, I was the kind of kid who needed to understand why things worked, not just that they did.

When I started my B.Tech in Computer Science with AI & ML at SITAMS, something clicked. I realized that data is essentially a language the world speaks — and most people can't hear it yet. My first real breakthrough came when I built a Banking Customer Churn Analysis system and watched Power BI dashboards reveal patterns that would take weeks to find manually. That moment — turning messy MySQL tables into actionable business insight — was the one that set my direction.

At Acenaar Technologies, I leveled up fast. I wasn't just analyzing data anymore — I was building AI systems. Designing an intelligent PDF Chatbot using RAG frameworks and vector databases taught me that the best data tools are invisible: they just answer your question.

Right now, I'm a fresher — but I've already shipped real systems, earned an ML certification from BHU via SWAYAM, and I'm deeply committed to one thing: making data speak clearly to the people who need it most. I'm looking for a team that believes insight should be democratized, not gatekept.
